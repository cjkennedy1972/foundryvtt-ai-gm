# Campaign Generation

AI-GM can create entire campaigns automatically or let you build them piece by piece. This document explains what gets generated and how you control the process.

## What Gets Generated

When you create a campaign, AI-GM generates:

### Settlements
Complete towns or cities with:
- **Name and appearance** — Distinctive visual identity
- **Population and size** — From small villages to large cities
- **Districts and landmarks** — Taverns, temples, shops, noble estates
- **Atmosphere and feel** — Bustling trade hub, sleepy fishing village, dangerous frontier town
- **Political structure** — Who's in charge and how

### NPCs
Characters with:
- **Names and appearances** — Memorable and fitting
- **Personalities and values** — How they act and what they believe
- **Backgrounds and history** — Why they're in the settlement
- **Relationships and conflicts** — How they relate to other NPCs
- **Secrets and motivations** — Hidden depths and goals

### Quests
Story hooks including:
- **Local problems** — Issues the settlement faces
- **Personal conflicts** — Disputes between NPCs
- **Mysteries to solve** — Disappearances, thefts, strange occurrences
- **Opportunities** — Ways to make money or allies
- **Consequences** — How quests connect to the larger world

### Lore
Backstory including:
- **Settlement history** — How the town was founded
- **Local legends** — Folk tales and myths
- **Political relationships** — Alliances and rivalries with other settlements
- **Supernatural elements** — Magic, curses, mysterious locations
- **Cultural values** — What the settlement cares about

## Customization Options

### Campaign Tone

Choose the overall feel:

| Tone | Style | Examples |
|------|-------|----------|
| **Heroic** | Epic, noble, inspiring | Lord of the Rings, Dragonlance |
| **Gritty** | Dark, morally complex, consequences matter | Game of Thrones, The Witcher |
| **Whimsical** | Playful, lighthearted, humor | Discworld, Baldur's Gate 3 (comedy mode) |
| **Horror** | Scary, unsettling, fear | Curse of Strahd, Ravenloft |
| **Mystery** | Puzzles, secrets, investigation | Sherlock Holmes, Murder mysteries |
| **Swashbuckling** | Adventure, action, treasure | Pirates of the Caribbean, Stardust |

### Setting & Theme

Choose a world style:

- **High Fantasy** — Magic is common, multiple races, kingdoms
- **Low Fantasy** — Magic is rare, grounded, realistic
- **Urban Fantasy** — Modern world with magic
- **Steampunk** — Industrial magic, clockwork, airships
- **Historical** — Real-world setting with fantasy elements
- **Custom** — Describe your own world

### Campaign Scope

Define how big the world is:

| Scope | Size | Focus |
|-------|------|-------|
| **Intimate** | One settlement | Deep community relationships |
| **Regional** | Several settlements | Local politics and conflicts |
| **Continental** | Multiple regions | Grand conspiracies |
| **Epic** | Entire world | World-shaking events |

### Content & Themes

Specify what you want:

**Include:**
- Specific genres (noir, romance, political intrigue)
- Themes (redemption, betrayal, corruption)
- Character types (mercenaries, scholars, rebels)
- Locations (underwater cities, sky islands, etc.)

**Avoid:**
- Specific content that's uncomfortable
- Themes you don't want
- Clichés you've played before

## Generation Process

### Quick Start

1. Click **Generate Campaign**
2. Choose tone and theme
3. Set campaign scope
4. Click **Generate**

AI-GM creates a complete campaign in seconds, ready to play.

### Guided Generation

For more control:

1. **Create Settlement** — Specify what kind of town you want
2. **Add NPCs** — Describe their role or let AI-GM create them
3. **Design Quests** — Pitch an idea, AI-GM develops it
4. **Weave Lore** — Connect elements together

This takes longer but gives you more creative input.

### Import Campaign

Bring in existing campaigns:

- D&D modules (Lost Mine of Phandelver, etc.)
- Homebrew campaigns (your own creations)
- Published settings (Waterdeep, Neverwinter, etc.)

AI-GM analyzes the campaign and enhances it with:
- Dynamic NPC behavior
- Living world mechanics
- Intelligent combat
- Semantic lore tracking

Your campaign gains AI benefits without losing what you've already built.

## What You Can Change

After generation, everything is editable:

### Change a Settlement
- Rename it
- Adjust population and size
- Modify atmosphere and architecture
- Add or remove districts
- Change political structure

### Modify an NPC
- Adjust personality and values
- Edit appearance and background
- Change relationships with others
- Update secrets and motivations
- Rename them

### Rewrite a Quest
- Change objectives and stakes
- Adjust rewards
- Modify quest-giver
- Alter consequences
- Make it easier or harder

### Edit Lore
- Add new backstory
- Modify relationships
- Change history
- Add legends or secrets
- Connect to other lore

## Generation Tips

**For best results:**

1. **Be specific about tone** — "Gritty mercenary campaign" better than "dark"
2. **Describe what you want NPCs to do** — "A morally ambiguous crime boss who secretly helps the poor"
3. **Set expectations about player agency** — "Player choices matter" vs. "Strongly scripted"
4. **Include niche themes** — "I want more LGBTQ+ representation" or "Include disability rep"
5. **Describe your party** — "New players who like roleplay" vs. "Min-maxers focused on combat"

**For ongoing campaigns:**

1. **Save between edits** — AI-GM remembers all changes
2. **Use lore annotations** — Mark what's important for the AI to remember
3. **Connect new elements to existing lore** — Make additions feel natural
4. **Test before playing** — Run a quick session to ensure tone matches

## Example Generation

### Request
"I want a campaign where the party manages a tavern in a coastal trading city. Tone should be lighthearted with serious moments. Include some pirates, merchants, and political intrigue."

### What Gets Generated

**Settlement:** Port Redhold
- Bustling coastal city with merchant ships and fishing boats
- Distinct districts: docks, merchant quarter, noble estates, slums
- Three rival merchant families competing for control

**NPCs:** 
- Marta Crane — Former pirate running the tavern (adventurous, protective)
- Lord Thayer — Noble merchant wanting to buy the tavern
- Captain Blackwood — Current pirate threat to the city
- Plus 12 other NPCs with roles and relationships

**Quests:**
- Protect the tavern from Lord Thayer's pressure
- Deal with a pirate captain targeting merchant ships
- Help a mysterious stranger with a hidden past
- Manage tavern rivalries and patrons

**Lore:**
- Port Redhold's history as a pirate haven
- Current political tensions between merchant families
- Legends of hidden treasure nearby
- Connections to wider world events

This entire world is ready to play in minutes, yet fully customizable.
